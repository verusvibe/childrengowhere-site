Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{cfg.privacyUpdatedLabel}}

ChildrenGoWhere helps parents and caregivers in Singapore find family-friendly places and events. This policy explains what the app and this website collect, why, and the choices you have.

In short

  • You can use the app as a guest, with no name or email.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.
  • Location is optional. It is used to show places near you and estimate travel time.
  • Analytics and crash reporting are on by default, and you can switch them off.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app, or by email if you cannot use the app. See how to delete your account.

1. Who we are

ChildrenGoWhere (“we”, “us”) is an app for iPhone, iPad and Android, and a website, for families in Singapore. If you have questions about this policy or your data, email {{cfg.contactEmail}}.

2. Information we collect

Account information

If you sign in with Apple or with email and password, we receive your name and email address. With Sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your email, in which case Apple gives us a private relay address. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us. On Android, Sign in with Apple opens in your browser.

If you continue as a guest, the app creates an anonymous account so that features work. We do not collect your name or email. Your favourites, collections and preferences are saved to that anonymous account in Firebase, so they are kept when you reopen the app, but they cannot be moved to another device or linked to you. Just opening and browsing the app, without choosing to continue as a guest, does not store anything about you on our servers.

Profile details you choose to add

Display name, phone number, country or region, and a profile photo. When you are signed in, your profile photo is also uploaded to Firebase Storage so it follows you to other devices.

Family members

You can add family members, including children, with a name, a relationship (for example child or spouse) and an age group. This is used to tailor suggestions to who is coming along. You manage these entries entirely yourself.

Things you save and your preferences

Favourites, collections, visited places, recently viewed places, preferred age range, activities, budget and location, and your notification choices. Recent searches are stored on your device. When you are signed in, saved items and preferences sync to your account so they follow you across devices.

Location

Only if you allow it, and only while you are using the app. See Location.

Calendar and photos

When you add an event to your calendar, the app never reads your existing events. On iPhone and iPad it asks permission to add events only. On Android it opens your calendar app with the event details filled in, and you choose whether to save it. When you pick a profile photo, the app receives only the photo you select through the system photo picker, not your whole library.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, the app registers your device with Firebase Cloud Messaging and subscribes it to the topics that match your notification preferences. On Android 13 and later the app asks for permission before showing notifications.

Analytics and crash reports

When Analytics is switched on, we collect usage events such as the places you view, how many results a search returned (but not what you searched for), and whether you signed in or continued as a guest. The iPhone and iPad app also records when you add or remove a favourite or ask for directions. Firebase also records technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version and crash reports. You can switch this off in Me > Privacy Settings.

We do not show ads. The app does not use your advertising identifier, and it does not track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Device checks and app settings

To block abuse and automated traffic, the app uses Firebase App Check, which has your device prove that it is running a genuine copy of the app (Apple App Attest on iPhone and iPad, Google Play Integrity on Android). This sends a device token to Apple or Google and to Firebase. The app also downloads settings, such as which features are switched on, from Firebase Remote Config, which uses a Firebase installation identifier.

This website

This website does not use cookies, advertising, or third-party analytics. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

3. How we use it

  • To show places and events that suit your family, and to find places near you
  • To sync your saved items and preferences across your devices
  • To personalise suggestions, if Personalisation is on
  • To fix crashes and improve the app, if Analytics is on
  • To send notifications you have asked for, and marketing notifications only if you opt in
  • To answer your support emails and keep the service secure

4. Location

If you allow location access, the app uses your device’s location to show nearby places and estimate travel time. It asks for a single location fix when needed rather than tracking you continuously. On Android you can allow approximate or precise location. You can turn this off with the Location Usage switch in Me > Privacy Settings, or in your device’s Settings > ChildrenGoWhere. Without location you can still browse and choose all of Singapore or one of its five regions instead. We save the area you choose as a preference.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and the app contains no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs. We use these service providers to run the app:

  • Google Firebase for sign-in, database, file storage, push notifications, analytics, crash reporting, App Check and Remote Config. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Maps and Google Play services on Android, for the map on place pages, directions and Play Integrity checks. Google receives standard technical data, such as your IP address, when a map is shown. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Apple for Sign in with Apple, Apple Maps directions on iPhone and iPad, App Attest and push notification delivery. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

6. Where it is stored

Your account data, including guest accounts, is stored with Google Firebase on Google Cloud infrastructure, which may be located outside Singapore. Profile photos are stored with Firebase Storage, and analytics and crash reports are processed by Google, which may also handle them in other countries. Recent searches stay on your device and are cleared when you sign out.

7. Your choices and rights

In Me > Privacy Settings you can control, independently:

  • Location Usage: on by default
  • Personalisation: on by default
  • Analytics: on by default. Turning it off stops analytics and crash reporting.
  • Marketing Notifications: off by default

You can also change notification preferences in Settings, edit your profile in Personal Information, use Export My Data to share a summary of your profile, preferences and saved places, and manage permissions in your device’s Settings.

If you are in Singapore, you can ask us to give you access to, or correct, personal data we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing {{cfg.contactEmail}}.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app under Me > Settings > Privacy Settings > Delete Account, on iPhone, iPad and Android. For your security you will be asked to confirm your password, or to confirm with Apple if you use Sign in with Apple. Deleting your account removes from our servers your sign-in, your profile and profile photo, your favourites, collections and saved preferences, and the family members you added. If you used Sign in with Apple, the app’s access to your Apple ID is also revoked. The analytics identifier on your device is reset.

If you cannot use the app, for example because you lost your device or forgot your password, you can ask us to delete your account instead. The steps are on our Delete your account page. We reply within 3 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days, after confirming that the request comes from the email address on the account.

Our database is backed up for disaster recovery. Deleted data can remain in those backups for up to 14 days, after which it is removed. Analytics and crash data that were already sent are kept according to Firebase’s retention settings and are not linked to your name or email.

Guest data is not linked to your name or email, so we usually cannot tell which guest account is yours, and the app may not offer a delete option for it. If you want a guest account removed, email us and we will look into it.

9. Children’s privacy

ChildrenGoWhere is designed for parents and caregivers, not for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. A parent or caregiver may add family members, including children, with a name, relationship and age group. You can edit or remove them at any time. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use Firebase security features, App Check and encrypted connections to protect your data. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your device up to date.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date at the top, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app or on this site.

12. Contact us

Questions or requests? Email {{cfg.contactEmail}}. You can also visit our Support page, or see how to delete your account. The rules for using the app are in our Terms of Service.
