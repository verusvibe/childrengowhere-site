Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{cfg.privacyUpdatedLabel}}

ChildrenGoWhere helps parents and caregivers in Singapore find family-friendly places and events. This policy explains what the app and this website collect, why, and the choices you have.

In short

  • You can use the app as a guest, with no name or email.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.
  • Location is optional. It is used to show places near you and estimate travel time.
  • Analytics and crash reporting are on by default, and you can switch them off.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app.

1. Who we are

ChildrenGoWhere (“we”, “us”) is an iPhone app and website for families in Singapore. If you have questions about this policy or your data, email {{cfg.contactEmail}}.

2. Information we collect

Account information

If you sign in with Apple or with email and password, we receive your name and email address. With Sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your email, in which case Apple gives us a private relay address. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.

If you continue as a guest, the app creates an anonymous account so that features work. We do not collect your name or email. Your favourites, collections and preferences are saved to that anonymous account in Firebase, so they are kept when you reopen the app, but they cannot be moved to another device or linked to you. Just opening and browsing the app, without choosing to continue as a guest, does not store anything about you on our servers.

Profile details you choose to add

Display name, phone number, country or region, and a profile photo. When you are signed in, your profile photo is also uploaded to Firebase Storage so it follows you to other devices.

Family members

You can add family members, including children, with a name, a relationship (for example child or spouse) and an age group. This is used to tailor suggestions to who is coming along. You manage these entries entirely yourself.

Things you save and your preferences

Favourites, collections, visited places, recently viewed places, preferred age range, activities, budget and location, and your notification choices. Recent searches are stored on your device. When you are signed in, saved items and preferences sync to your account so they follow you across devices.

Location

Only if you allow it, and only while you are using the app. See Location.

Calendar and photos

When you add an event, the app asks for permission to add events to your calendar. It has write-only access and cannot read your existing calendar. When you pick a profile photo, the app receives only the photo you select, not your whole library.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, the app registers your device with Firebase Cloud Messaging and subscribes it to the topics that match your notification preferences.

Analytics and crash reports

When Analytics is switched on, we collect usage events such as the places you view, favourites you add or remove, how many results a search returned (but not what you searched for), directions requests, and whether you signed in or continued as a guest. Firebase also records technical information such as device model, iOS version, app version and crash reports. You can switch this off in Me > Privacy Settings.

This website

This website does not use cookies, advertising, or third-party analytics. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

3. How we use it

  • To show places and events that suit your family, and to find places near you
  • To sync your saved items and preferences across your devices
  • To personalise suggestions, if Personalisation is on
  • To fix crashes and improve the app, if Analytics is on
  • To send notifications you have asked for, and marketing notifications only if you opt in
  • To answer your support emails and keep the service secure

4. Location

If you allow location access, the app uses your iPhone’s location to show nearby places and estimate travel time. It asks for a single location fix when needed rather than tracking you continuously. You can turn this off with the Location Usage switch in Me > Privacy Settings, or in iPhone Settings > ChildrenGoWhere. Without location you can still browse and choose a location manually.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and the app contains no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs. We use these service providers to run the app:

  • Google Firebase for sign-in, database, file storage, push notifications, analytics and crash reporting. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Apple for Sign in with Apple, Apple Maps directions and push notification delivery. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

6. Where it is stored

Your account data, including guest accounts, is stored in Firebase’s database in Google Cloud’s Singapore region. Profile photos are stored with Firebase Storage, and analytics and crash reports are processed by Google; these may be handled in countries other than Singapore. Recent searches stay on your device and are cleared when you sign out.

7. Your choices and rights

In Me > Privacy Settings you can control, independently:

  • Location Usage: on by default
  • Personalisation: on by default
  • Analytics: on by default. Turning it off stops analytics and crash reporting.
  • Marketing Notifications: off by default

You can also change notification preferences in Settings, edit your profile in Personal Information, use Export My Data to share a summary of your profile, preferences and saved places, and manage permissions in iPhone Settings.

If you are in Singapore, you can ask us to give you access to, or correct, personal data we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing {{cfg.contactEmail}}.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app under Me > Settings > Privacy Settings > Delete Account. For your security you will be asked to confirm your password, or to confirm with Apple if you use Sign in with Apple. Deleting your account permanently removes from our servers your sign-in, your profile and profile photo, your favourites, collections and saved preferences, and the family members you added. If you used Sign in with Apple, the app’s access to your Apple ID is also revoked. Your device’s notification registration is cancelled and the analytics identifier on your device is reset.

Analytics and crash data that were already sent are kept according to Firebase’s retention settings and are not linked to your name or email. Guest accounts have no delete option in the app; if you used the app as a guest and want that data erased, email us and we will remove it.

9. Children’s privacy

ChildrenGoWhere is designed for parents and caregivers, not for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. A parent or caregiver may add family members, including children, with a name, relationship and age group. You can edit or remove them at any time. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use Firebase security features and encrypted connections to protect your data. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your device up to date.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date at the top, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app or on this site.

12. Contact us

Questions or requests? Email {{cfg.contactEmail}}. You can also visit our Support page. The rules for using the app are in our Terms of Service.
