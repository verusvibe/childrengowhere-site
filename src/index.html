Why this metric

Browsing and filtering are means, not ends. The moment of real value is when a family decides “we’re going here” and asks for directions. That is a stronger signal than a search or a save, and it maps to an event the app already logs (directions_requested).

Guardrail metrics

These should stay healthy while the North Star grows:

  • Crash-free rate, from Crashlytics
  • Week-2 retention, from Firebase Analytics
  • Search relevance, for example searches that lead to a place view

This is a proposed metric, not an adopted company target. “Instrumented today” means the app already logs the event through Firebase Analytics. Figures only include people who leave Analytics switched on in Privacy Settings.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

Is ChildrenGoWhere free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Which places does it cover?

ChildrenGoWhere is built for families in Singapore. The places and events in the app are Singapore locations.

Do I need an account?

No. You can continue as a guest and start exploring straight away. Signing in with Apple or email lets your favourites, collections and family members sync across your devices.

How does it help me find the right age and budget?

Browse by age band (from babies to teens) or by budget (Free, under $10, under $20 or premium), and use quick filters such as Rainy Day, Indoor, Outdoor, Kid-Friendly and Birthday Party. Advanced filters let you narrow by distance, category and price.

Does the app use my location?

Only if you allow it. Location is used to show places near you and estimate travel time. You can switch it off at any time in Me > Privacy Settings, or in your iPhone’s Settings.

Will it read my calendar?

No. When you add an event, ChildrenGoWhere asks permission to add it to your calendar. It cannot read your existing events.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone running iOS 18 or later.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Me > Settings > Privacy Settings > Delete Account. If you need help, or want us to confirm that your saved data has been erased, email {{cfg.contactEmail}}.
